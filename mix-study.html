The DCIMIT project, launched in January 2025, examines disparities in the prevention and early diagnosis of cervical cancer among immigrant women in Italy through three complementary studies

Study 1

Mixed Method Study

This study aims to investigate the factors that influence access to early diagnosis and treatment among women born in countries with high migration pressure who have been diagnosed with cervical cancer or high-grade cervical lesions, by combining quantitative and qualitative data.

Specific Objectives

1. Collect quantitative data on perceived factors that may hinder or facilitate access to early diagnosis and treatment

2. Conduct a qualitative exploration of the participants’ experiences related to prevention, diagnosis, and treatment

3. Integrate quantitative and qualitative data to gain a deeper understanding of the factors that influence access to services and care

Methods

DESIGN: a multicenter study using a mixed-methods approach, with a quantitative component based on a questionnaire and a qualitative component based on individual interviews

CENTERS: 10 gynecology centers across Italy

PARTICIPANTS: 300 women born in countries with high migratory pressure who have been diagnosed with cervical cancer or high-grade cervical lesions; a subgroup of 60 participants will take part in individual interviews

Eligibility Criteria:

- Age above 18

- A diagnosis of cervical cancer or high-grade squamous intraepithelial lesion (HSIL) received in Italy within the last 5 years

- Born in a country facing significant migration pressure

- Current residence in Italy
