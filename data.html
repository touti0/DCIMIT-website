Glossary

Learn terms and concepts regarding the project's topic

01

Immigrant, migrant, and people with a migration background

in the DICE protocols, we used the term “immigrant,” but I would also include the other two terms, as they are often used interchangeably.

02

Hard-to-reach populations and underserved populations

Terms used in public health, social research, and policy to describe groups that face significant barriers to engagement, representation, or resources, though they emphasize slightly different structural issues.

03

Equity

The project is also grounded in a social justice perspective.

04

Social determinants of health

A framework that goes beyond the biomedical approach.

05

Intersectionality

Our population experiences the intersection of two key determinants, gender and migration.

05

Transcultural health

One aim is to promote health and culturally sensitive health services.
