Gynecological Exam

For foreign women at the Immigrant Women's Center and for young women aged 14-19 at the Youth Center, access is direct and no appointment is necessary. A health insurance card is required. For gynecological exams and consultations, whether at the specialist clinic or at the counseling services, a health insurance card is sufficient, and no doctor's referral is required.

Pap-test Screening

A Pap test is a screening test for the prevention and early diagnosis of cervical cancer and precancerous lesions. A small sample of cells is taken from the cervix to be examined under a microscope. This test is part of the screening program for the prevention and early diagnosis of cervical cancer, which covers women aged 25 to 29, and women aged 30 to 64 only during the transition phase (2016–2018), as the program will be gradually replaced by an HPV test every five years for this age group.
