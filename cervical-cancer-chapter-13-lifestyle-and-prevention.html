Lifestyle & Prevention

What actually helps — and what it can't replace.

Press play below to begin ▶

01

Smoking

Smoking makes it harder for the immune system to clear HPV, raising risk. Quitting measurably helps.

02

HPV vaccination

The single most effective prevention tool available, when eligible — it prevents the infections that cause most cervical cancers.

03

Safer sex & immune health

Condoms lower HPV transmission risk (without eliminating it), and general immune health supports the body's ability to clear infections naturally.

04

Screening — the constant

None of the above replaces regular screening. It remains the most reliable way to catch problems before they become cancer.

Key takeaways

The honest takeaway

Lifestyle choices help. Not smoking and general health support your immune system.

Vaccination and screening do the heavy lifting. They're the two tools with the strongest evidence.

No lifestyle guarantees safety. Even the healthiest habits don't replace screening.

Combine what you can control with the two things that matter most: vaccination and screening.

General information, not medical advice.
