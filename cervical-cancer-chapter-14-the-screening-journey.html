01 · The path

From invitation to follow-up

A letter arrives, an appointment gets booked, a short clinic visit happens — and within a few weeks, there's an answer. Most journeys end right there, with normal results.

02

What each step feels like

  • Invitation: a reminder, not an alarm
  • Booking: a five-minute call or a few clicks online
  • Clinic: a short wait, a private room, a quick conversation
  • Test: under a minute of mild discomfort
  • Results: arrive by mail, message, or a follow-up call

Key takeaways

The takeaway

It's a short process. From invitation to result, usually just a few weeks.

Most journeys end simply. A normal result — then the same reminder again in a few years.

Every step is manageable. None of it requires more than an hour of your day.

The hardest part is usually just picking up the phone to book it.

General information, not medical advice.
