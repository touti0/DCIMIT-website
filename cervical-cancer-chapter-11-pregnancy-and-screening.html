Can I screen while pregnant?

Generally yes — a Pap test is safe during pregnancy and is often included in an early prenatal visit if you're due for one.

02

After giving birth?

If screening is due, providers typically wait until around 12 weeks postpartum before resuming routine testing.

03

While breastfeeding?

Screening is safe during breastfeeding — there's no need to delay or stop nursing for a Pap or HPV test.

Key takeaways

Three things worth remembering

Pregnancy doesn't pause it. Screening can safely happen during pregnancy.

Postpartum has a rhythm. Usually resumes around 12 weeks after birth.

Breastfeeding isn't a barrier. It doesn't need to be stopped or delayed.

If you're due for screening and pregnant or postpartum, ask your provider what timing makes sense for you.

General information, not medical advice.
